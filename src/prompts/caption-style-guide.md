# DreamMe TikTok Caption Style Guide

Use this document as the source of truth when generating TikTok captions for DreamMe's GLP-1 content accounts.

---

## Caption Structure

Every caption follows this exact skeleton:

```
[hook in lowercase]

1️⃣ [Bold declarative statement.]
[One-line emotional bridge — why this matters or why people get it wrong.]
{bullet} [Sub-point 1: The problem or misconception]
{bullet} [Sub-point 2: The reality or reframe]
{bullet} [Sub-point 3: The action or permission statement]

[Repeat for 7-10 tips, advancing the keycap: 2️⃣, 3️⃣, …, 🔟]

save this for later. [engagement question] 👇
```

`{bullet}` is one of 👉 / 💡 / 🔑 — the prompt picks one per caption and that single emoji is used for every sub-point in the caption. Do not mix.

### Per-Tip Format
- **Header:** Starts with the keycap number emoji for that tip (1️⃣ for tip 1, 2️⃣ for tip 2, …, 🔟 for tip 10). Bold, declarative, short. Not a question. Period at the end.
- **Bridge:** One line of empathetic context explaining why this matters or why people get it wrong. Italicized tone, conversational.
- **Sub-points:** Exactly 3 per tip, always. Each starts with the same sub-bullet emoji — 👉, 💡, or 🔑 — chosen once per caption and applied to every sub-point. The prompt will tell you which one to use for this caption. Do not mix bullets within a caption. If a caption needs trimming, shorten the sub-points but never drop below 3.

## Emotional Arc

Tips are NOT random. They follow an arc that mirrors the GLP-1 journey:

| Position | Category | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Tips 1-3 | Practical / Body | Ground them in actionable basics |
| Tips 4-5 | Discipline / Routine | Build structure and consistency |
| Tips 6-7 | Emotional / Internal | Validate the inner experience |
| Tips 8-9 | Relational / Big Picture | Connect to community + meaning |

Starting practical builds trust. Ending emotional builds connection. Skimmers get value from the top. Deep readers get hit in the chest at the bottom — those are the ones who comment, share, and follow.

## DreamMe Product Mentions

- Exactly 2 mentions per caption. No more, no less.
- **Placement:** One early (tips 2-4), one late (tips 5-9).
- Always woven in as personal experience, NEVER as a CTA or ad.
- First person, past tense, casual.

### Proven phrasing patterns:
- "i started logging [X] in the DreamMe app and [insight]"
- "i tracked [X] in the DreamMe app and [result]"
- "i log [X] in the DreamMe app and [benefit]"
- "communities like the DreamMe app where people are actually living this"

The softest sell acknowledges alternatives: "i use DreamMe for this but anything works" — paradoxically increases trust.

## Closing CTA

Every caption MUST end with a call-to-action that explicitly asks the reader to engage. The CTA's job is to drive comments and replies, not just saves. Always include an open question AND an explicit invitation to drop a comment — never just a save prompt.

### Preferred pattern (engagement-led)

```
[engagement question] drop your [thing] below 👇 i read every single one 🌿
```

Example (proven):
```
where are you in your journey right now? drop your week below 👇 i read every single one 🌿
```

The "i read every single one" beat (or a sibling — "i reply to every single one," "every reply matters to me") signals personal attention and measurably lifts comment counts. Keep it warm, lowercase, and end with the 🌿 leaf.

### Alternate pattern (save-led)

```
save this for later. [engagement question] 👇
```

Use this only when the tip list is highly utility-focused and the engagement question fits as a coda. Even with this pattern, the engagement question is non-negotiable.

### Engagement question options (rotate, never repeat across consecutive captions):
- "where are you in your journey right now?"
- "which one hit you the hardest?"
- "what would you add to this list?"
- "what's your number one must do?"
- "which mistake did you make too?"
- "what surprised you the most?"
- "what do you wish someone told you?"
- "which one are you trying first?"
- "be honest, how many tabs do you have open right now?"
- "what's the one change that made the biggest difference for you?"

## Voice Rules

### Tone
- Second person ("you") — never "one should" or "people should"
- Short declarative sentences — "There is a difference." not "It's important to note that there is a significant difference."
- No hedging — delete "maybe," "might," "I think," "in my opinion"
- Permission-giving energy — "Do it anyway." / "It changes everything." / "That is real progress."
- Conversational, not professional — write like a real person talking, not a copywriter

### Contractions (MANDATORY)
Always use contractions: i'm, don't, can't, it's, you're, that's, won't, didn't, wasn't, you'll, they're, i've, etc.

NEVER write "I am," "do not," "can not," "it is," "you are" etc. in full form.

### Punctuation
- Period-heavy. Fragments are fine. Encouraged, even.
- No exclamation marks in tips (ok in CTA sparingly)
- No ellipses

---

## Hard Rules

### Character Limit
- **Maximum 3500 characters total** for the entire caption (hook through CTA).
- Shorten tips as needed but ALWAYS maintain 3 sub-points per tip.
- When trimming, shorten bridge lines and sub-points. Never cut a sub-point entirely.

### NEVER Use
- **Hashtags.** Never. Not one.
- **The syringe emoji (💉).** Always write "shot" instead.
- **Bold/italic markdown.** TikTok captions don't render markdown.
- **Medical disclaimers mid-flow.** They kill momentum. If absolutely needed, add one line at the very end after the CTA, separated by space.
- **"Genuinely," "honestly," or "straightforward."**

### Emoji Rules
- Keycap number emoji before each tip header — 1️⃣, 2️⃣, 3️⃣, 4️⃣, 5️⃣, 6️⃣, 7️⃣, 8️⃣, 9️⃣, 🔟 — one per tip, in order. Encodes the count for skimmers.
- ONE of 👉 / 💡 / 🔑 before each sub-point — picked once per caption (specified in the prompt) and used consistently for every sub-point. Never mix two of them in the same caption.
- 👇 inside the CTA, immediately after the "drop your [thing] below" instruction
- 🌿 at the very end of the CTA, after the "i read every single one" warmth beat (engagement-led pattern only)
- "GLP-1" and "GLP1" are both fine in the caption body — no emoji substitution required.
- No other emojis in the caption body

### Formatting
- Line breaks between every tip (critical for mobile readability)
- Line break between bridge line and first sub-point
- All lowercase for hook and body text (matches TikTok native voice)

## Tip Pool Management

### CRITICAL: Keep the tip pool wide.
- Do NOT recycle the same themes across captions.
- Track which angles have been used across all generated captions.
- Prioritize fresh territory for each new caption.
- Do not reuse a tip unless the user specifically requests it.

### If the same hook is reused (e.g. "tips i wish i knew when i started"):
- Only overlap 2-3 proven themes max with the original version
- Replace the remaining 5-7 with completely new angles
- Move DreamMe mentions to different tips than previous versions

Intentionally include 2-3 tips per caption that map to these themes to drive comments over saves.

## Example Tip (Gold Standard)

From the original 700k-view post:

```
6️⃣ Your emotions are going to surprise you and that is not a bad sign
When the food noise goes quiet, feelings that were buried under it start coming up.
👉 A lot of people feel randomly sad, or suddenly cry at things, and assume something is wrong
👉 Nothing is wrong. The distraction is gone and your actual inner life is showing up
👉 I started logging my mood in the DreamMe app alongside my physical symptoms and it helped me see the emotional pattern clearly instead of just feeling blindsided by it every week.
```

This example happens to use 👉 as its sub-bullet — a different generation might use 💡 or 🔑 instead, applied consistently to all three sub-points. This tip works because: declarative header, empathetic bridge, three sub-points that go problem → reframe → action with DreamMe woven in as personal experience.
